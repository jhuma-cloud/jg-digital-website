Local SEO Checklist: Get Found on Google Maps in 2026

If customers search "near me" or "[service] in [city]", local SEO determines whether you show up — or your competitor does. Use this checklist to build a strong local presence.

Google Business Profile

  • Claim and verify your listing
  • Use accurate name, address, and phone (NAP)
  • Add photos, services, and business hours
  • Post updates at least twice a month
  • Reply to every review within 48 hours

On-page local signals

Create location-specific pages if you serve multiple areas. Include city names naturally in titles, meta descriptions, and headings — not stuffed, but clear.

Citations and consistency

Your business name, address, and phone should match everywhere — website footer, directories, social profiles. Inconsistency confuses Google.

Reviews matter

Ask happy customers for Google reviews. Volume, recency, and responses all influence map pack rankings. Never buy fake reviews.

Local content

Blog about local topics: case studies, community events, area-specific tips. It signals relevance to your region.
