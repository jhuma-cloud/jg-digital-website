WhatsApp Marketing for Small Business: A Practical Starter Guide

WhatsApp has over 2 billion users. For many businesses — especially in India — it's the primary channel customers prefer. Here's how to use it professionally without spamming.

Start with WhatsApp Business

Download WhatsApp Business (free). Set up your profile with logo, description, hours, and catalog if you sell products. Use a dedicated business number, not your personal one.

Quick replies and away messages

Save templates for common questions: pricing, location, hours. Set an away message for off-hours so leads know when to expect a reply.

Broadcast lists (carefully)

Only message people who opted in. Share value — offers, tips, updates — not daily sales pitches. Keep broadcasts under 256 contacts per list and segment by interest.

Automate follow-ups

When someone fills a form or clicks your website WhatsApp button, trigger an instant greeting: "Thanks for reaching out — how can we help?" Tools like WhatsApp Business API or CRM integrations scale this.

Measure what works

Track how many chats become sales. Tag conversations by source (website, ads, referral). Double down on channels that bring quality leads.
