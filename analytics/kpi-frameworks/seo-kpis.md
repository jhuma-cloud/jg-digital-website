# SEO — KPI framework

| KPI | Definition | Source | Frequency |
|-----|------------|--------|-----------|
| Organic sessions | Sessions from organic search | Analytics | Monthly |
| Non-brand clicks | Clicks on non-branded queries | Search Console | Monthly |
| Conversions (organic) | Goal completions from organic | Analytics | Monthly |
| Top keyword visibility | Share of tracked keywords in top 10 | Rank tracker | Monthly |

## Targets (example — customize per client)

| Tier | Organic session growth MoM |
|------|----------------------------|
| Baseline | 0–3% |
| Good | 3–8% |
| Strong | 8%+ |

## Notes

Document seasonality and algorithm update impacts in client `reports/`.
