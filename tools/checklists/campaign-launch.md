# Campaign launch checklist

Use before any paid or major organic campaign goes live.

## Strategy and setup

- [ ] Objectives and KPIs documented (`analytics/kpi-frameworks/`)
- [ ] Tracking verified (pixels, UTMs, conversion events)
- [ ] Budget and flight dates confirmed with client
- [ ] Creative brief approved (`templates/creative-brief.md`)

## Creative and copy

- [ ] Assets match `creative/ad-specs/`
- [ ] Copy proofread; legal/disclaimers included
- [ ] Landing page live and mobile-tested

## Platform

- [ ] Campaign structure built in ad platform
- [ ] Audiences and exclusions set
- [ ] Naming convention applied for reporting

## Go-live

- [ ] Test conversion or preview approved
- [ ] Client sign-off (if required)
- [ ] Launch logged in `clients/{slug}/campaigns/`

## Post-launch (48h)

- [ ] Delivery and spend pacing checked
- [ ] No critical tracking errors
- [ ] Quick win optimizations noted
